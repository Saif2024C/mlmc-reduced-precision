ABSOLUTE RATES, 1 thread, uniform bits (RNG/s)
  ours:  thesis 3.11e9 | mimic idle 1.705e9 | nocturne 3.099e9
  MKL :  thesis 2.93e9 | mimic idle 1.330e9 | nocturne 1.292e9

FINDINGS
  1. The QUALITATIVE conclusions of Tables 2.2/2.3 hold on idle mimic:
     in-register wins on raw bits, loses once a transform is applied,
     and loses to the cheap generators at 1 thread (0.78-0.82).
  2. But the NUMBERS move. 1-thread uniform bits 1.06 -> 1.28;
     16-thread 1.87 -> 2.66. Table 2.3's 1-thread values move
     0.55-0.60 -> 0.78-0.82.
  3. Absolute rates on idle mimic are ~1.8x LOWER than the thesis run
     for BOTH sides (ours 3.11e9 -> 1.705e9). Both scale together, so
     the ratios survive, but the thesis run was evidently at a higher
     clock than an idle machine reaches now. Unexplained.
  4. nocturne remains the outlier: MKL is much slower there while our
     generator is unchanged, so every ratio inflates.

RECOMMENDATION: the thesis numbers are reproducible in DIRECTION but
not in VALUE. If the tables are re-run, re-run all rows in one session
on one machine and record the load.
