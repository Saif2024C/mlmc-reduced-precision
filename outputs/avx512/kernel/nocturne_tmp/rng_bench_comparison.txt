KEY DIFFERENCES
  1. In-register generator looks MUCH better on nocturne. On mimic it LOST
     to all three cheap generators at 1 thread (0.55-0.60x); on nocturne it
     WINS against every one at every thread count (1.13-2.81x).
  2. Our raw uniform-bit rate is nearly identical on both machines
     (3.10e9 vs 3.11e9 at 1 thread) -- it is MKL that is slower on nocturne
     (1.29e9 vs 2.93e9), so the gap is MKL's, not ours.
  3. FP32 Normal at 16 threads: 1.09x on mimic -> 1.85x on nocturne.
  4. FP16 Normal is the one stage unchanged: 0.88 vs 0.85, MKL ahead on
     both machines.

CAVEAT: different CPU (Granite vs Emerald Rapids) AND different load.
Cannot separate the two causes from these runs alone.
